About

Hi!
I'm Nat, a third-year mechanical engineering student at RIT, pursuing a master's degree with a dual focus in aerospace and computer engineering. From building Lego robots in elementary school, to programming CNC mills for precision parts in high school, to leading the construction of a soft-body robotic tentacle. My life has been a series of projects, and this website is an attempt to document its highlights.

Throughout my life I have been fascinated by the technical world around me, and have loved learning everything I can. I have pushed myself to pursue as many engineering areas as possible and focused on being interdisciplinary.
